About this model & data

The original exterior shell has 1,138 faces and six static property rows. Geometry, cell count, well positions and source model files are preserved. Lighting and annotations add visual clarity, not geological resolution.

Per-cell readouts are approximate values reconstructed from the original 8-bit property texture. They are not full-precision simulator output. Depth is cell-centre TVD in feet, not well measured depth.

Animation day now follows a calculated, assumption-based oil–water transport scenario. This is not measured production data or a validated field forecast. Only saturations evolve; rock properties stay static. The complete assumptions and numerical checks are below.

Assumptions & numerical checks

1 PV = the model's normalized pore volume, not barrels. Approximate source porosity weights equal-volume cells. Six-neighbor connections use reconstructed PERMX in all directions.

Before day 7, pure-water injection is 0.02 PV/day at INJ and three producers withdraw equally. From day 7, PROD-3 is shut; PROD-1 and PROD-2 retain their original rates and injection equals their exact sum (⅔ × 0.02 PV/day). All active wells connect equally to five layers. Closed outer boundary. Incompressible oil/water, equal viscosity, linear relative permeability. No gravity, capillarity, gas, trapping or polymer.

Pressure, physical volumes, PVT, full internal geometry, measured completions and restart history were not supplied. The flow potential is a graph calculation; no field pressure or calibrated travel time is reported.

Water + oil saturation = 1. Upwind steps use CFL ≤ 0.8. Phase balance error: —. Numerical consistency is not field validation.
