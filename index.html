About this model & data

The original exterior shell has 1,138 faces and six static property rows. Geometry, cell count, well positions and source model files are preserved. Lighting and annotations add visual clarity, not geological resolution.

Per-cell readouts are approximate values reconstructed from the original 8-bit property texture. They are not full-precision simulator output. Depth is cell-centre TVD in feet, not well measured depth.

Animation days and well-flow motion are a visual demonstration, not measured production data or a validated reservoir simulation. Day spacing and movement speed are not calibrated. Production history and restart frames were not supplied; initial oil saturation is EQUIL-derived.
